Pines USA Biodiesel

Off-Road Biodiesel for
Working Operations

Pines USA produces off-road biodiesel from used cooking oil recovered by Pines Green. The product is intended for agricultural and industrial non-road diesel applications.

Our Product

Off-Road Biodiesel Built
for Working Land

Farms, ranches, and construction operations depend on diesel to work. Tractors, irrigation pumps, hay equipment, skid steers, and generators all run on it. Pines USA produces an off-road biodiesel as an alternative for those non-road applications.

The product is derived from used cooking oil recovered by Pines Green from restaurants and food service operations, then processed at the group's Athens, Texas facility. Feedstock, production, and delivery all stay under the group's control.

Off-road fuel is not subject to the same on-highway tax structure as pump diesel, which matters for high-volume users. Contact us to discuss current pricing and availability. If you also need conventional diesel, gasoline, or DEF, Pines Fuel Brokerage can quote those alongside it.

Product Information

What We Produce

The product is produced from used cooking oil feedstock and processed through transesterification. The following is a general overview. Contact us for full specification and batch documentation.

B100

Blend Grade

Our base product is B100, meaning it is 100 percent biodiesel by volume. It can be blended with petroleum diesel at various ratios depending on your equipment requirements and operating conditions.

Off-Road

Intended Application

This fuel is classified for off-road use only. It is appropriate for farm tractors, irrigation equipment, generators, skid steers, and other non-highway diesel machinery.

Recovered

Feedstock Origin

Feedstock is used cooking oil recovered by Pines Green from restaurants, commercial kitchens, schools, and food service operations. Collection and production sit under the same group.

Athens, TX

Processing Location

Production takes place at the group's processing facility in Athens, Texas. All processing is done in house under direct operational control.

Flexible

Regional

Why Biodiesel

Considerations for
Commercial Buyers

  • Domestically Produced
    Feedstock is recovered and processed in the United States. There is no imported product in the supply chain.
  • Off-Road Tax Considerations
    Off-road fuel is subject to different tax treatment than highway diesel. For high-volume farm operations, this is a factor worth evaluating with your accountant.
  • High Lubricity
    Biodiesel is well known for its superior lubricity properties compared to ultra-low-sulfur petroleum diesel, which can benefit injection pump and fuel system components in older equipment.
  • Controlled Supply Chain
    The group collects the feedstock, processes it, and delivers the finished product. Fewer parties in the chain means fewer places for a delivery to fall apart.
