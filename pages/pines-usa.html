Overview

Biodiesel Made From Recovered Cooking Oil

Pines USA takes the waste cooking oil recovered by Pines Solutions and produces biodiesel at our Athens, Texas facility. The finished fuel is supplied as pure B100 or blended with petroleum diesel to the ratio a buyer specifies.

Because the oil comes from our own collection operation, the supply behind every batch is under our control. We know where it came from, how it was handled, and how it was cleaned before production.

Our buyers run diesel equipment every day and buy in working volumes: farms and ranches, fleets, contractors, and industrial operations.

Biodiesel Blends

A blend is named for its biodiesel percentage. B100 is pure biodiesel. B20 is 20 percent biodiesel and 80 percent petroleum diesel. Pines USA supplies the full range, and we will recommend a blend based on your equipment, the season, and how the fuel will be stored.

Blend Composition Common Use
B100 100% biodiesel Pure biodiesel for equipment and storage suited to it, and as blending stock for buyers who blend on site.
B50 50% biodiesel, 50% petroleum diesel High-content blend for warm-season use in compatible equipment.
B20 20% biodiesel, 80% petroleum diesel The most widely used blend for farm equipment, fleets, and construction machinery.
B10 10% biodiesel, 90% petroleum diesel A moderate blend for colder months or equipment with tighter blend limits.
B5 5% biodiesel, 95% petroleum diesel Broad equipment compatibility with added lubricity.
B2 2% biodiesel, 98% petroleum diesel Adds lubricity to diesel with minimal change to other fuel properties.
Custom Ratio set to your specification Seasonal adjustments, specific equipment requirements, or blends not listed here.

Check your equipment manufacturer's guidance on approved biodiesel blends before purchasing. Contact us for current availability and pricing.

Production

How Our Biodiesel Is Made

Production runs in house at the Athens facility under our direct control, from the moment oil arrives to the moment fuel leaves.

  1. Intake

    Cleaned oil arrives from Pines Solutions and is inspected for water and contamination. Oil that does not meet our intake standards is rejected.

  2. Production

    Accepted oil is converted into biodiesel at our Athens, Texas facility.

  3. Testing

    Each batch is checked before release, and the results are recorded per batch.

  4. Blending and Supply

    Fuel is supplied as B100 or blended to your specification, with volumes documented on every delivery or pickup.

Quality and Documentation

Records You Can Check

Batch records cover the oil source, production date, test results, and release status. If you run biodiesel in newer equipment, in cold conditions, or under a warranty requirement, you can request that documentation before you buy.

We will also tell you when biodiesel is not the right fit. Cold weather, storage time, and certain engines all have real limits, and we would rather set the blend correctly than sell you a problem.

Why Buyers Choose Biodiesel

  • Domestically produced. Recovered and processed in Texas, with no imported product in the chain.
  • Higher lubricity. Biodiesel lubricates fuel system parts better than ultra-low sulfur diesel alone, which helps older injection pumps.
  • Recycled feedstock. Made from cooking oil that would otherwise need to be disposed of.
  • Controlled supply. One company collects the oil, produces the fuel, and delivers it.
Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use biodiesel in my existing diesel equipment?

Many diesel engines run on biodiesel blends with little or no modification, and most manufacturers approve lower blends such as B5. Approval for higher blends varies by make and model year. Check your manufacturer's documentation and talk to us about your equipment before you buy.

What is the difference between B100 and a blend?

B100 is pure biodiesel with no petroleum diesel in it. A blend such as B20 is 20 percent biodiesel and 80 percent petroleum diesel. Blends perform differently across temperatures and equipment types, and we will recommend a ratio for your operation.

How does cold weather affect biodiesel?

Biodiesel begins to cloud and thicken at higher temperatures than petroleum diesel. In Texas winters this is usually manageable, especially with lower blends and proper storage. Ask us before the season changes rather than after.

Is there a minimum purchase?

We work with buyers of different sizes. Contact us to discuss current availability and what arrangement makes sense for your volume. We will talk through logistics before you commit to anything.

How is biodiesel priced?

Pricing depends on blend, volume, delivery location, and current market conditions, so we do not publish a fixed price list. Contact us for a current quote.

Do you deliver, or do buyers pick up?

Both are available depending on your location and volume. We will confirm the options for your site when you request a quote.

I have waste cooking oil. Can I supply it instead?

Yes. Oil collection is handled by our sister company, Pines Solutions, through scheduled pickup or the Recovery Point. See Pines Solutions to get started.
