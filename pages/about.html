About the Group

Pines Energy Group LLC

A privately held energy company built around four operating divisions. We supply fuel, produce it, recover the feedstock behind it, and hold the businesses that do the work.

Who We Are

A Group Built
Around Fuel Supply

Pines Energy Group LLC is headquartered in Athens, Texas. The company began as a single production operation converting used cooking oil into biodiesel and has since grown into a group covering commercial fuel procurement, oil and gas, recycling, and business ownership.

The structure came out of the work rather than a plan on paper. Producing biodiesel required a steady feedstock supply, which became a collection operation. Selling fuel to commercial accounts meant buyers started asking for products we did not produce, which became a brokerage. Owning several businesses under one roof required a holding company, which became Pines Holdings.

Each division has its own customers, its own operations, and its own point of contact. What they share is a customer base that buys fuel and services in commercial volumes and expects both to arrive when promised.

Operating Philosophy

How We Run
the Business

Fuel is not a differentiated product. Diesel from one terminal is the same as diesel from another. What separates suppliers is whether the delivery happens, whether the price was fair, and whether someone answers when a problem comes up.

Contact the Group
01

Do What Was Agreed

A delivery window is a commitment, not an estimate. When we cannot hit one, the customer hears it from us before they notice it themselves.

02

Price It Openly

Quotes show what is product, what is freight, and what is margin. Customers who understand how a price is built make better purchasing decisions.

03

Keep the Structure Flat

Small teams, direct lines, and decisions made by people who know the account. No layers between the customer and someone who can act.

04

Own the Whole Chain

Where we can control an input, we do. Running our own collection operation is what makes production consistent, and consistency is what customers actually buy.

05

Say No When It Fits

If biodiesel is wrong for your equipment or the volume does not justify a route, we will tell you. Selling the wrong thing once costs more than the order was worth.

06

Document the Work

Collection records, batch results, and delivery volumes are kept and provided on request. Customers should be able to verify what they were told.

Service Standards

Commitment to
Dependable Service

Everything the group sells is something a customer needs on a schedule. A restaurant needs the container emptied before it overflows. A contractor needs equipment fueled before the crew starts. A fleet needs product in the tank before the trucks roll.

  • Confirmed Schedules
    Every recurring account runs on a set frequency with a known window. Changes are communicated before the date, not after it.
  • Backup Supply
    More than one qualified supplier per product and region, so a terminal outage or a carrier problem does not become your problem.
  • Direct Contact
    You reach the division handling your account. No routing through a call center and no ticket numbers.
  • Records on Request
    Delivery volumes, collection manifests, and batch documentation are kept and provided when you need them for your own files.
  • Capacity Before Commitment
    We take on accounts we can actually service. Growth that outruns the equipment and the crew shows up as missed deliveries.
Growth Strategy

How the Group Expands

Growth follows the customers we already have. When an account asks for a product or a service we do not offer, that is the signal to look at adding it. It is a slower way to expand than chasing new markets, and it puts revenue behind the decision before the capital goes out.

Three paths carry the group forward. Deeper coverage of existing markets, which means more collection routes, more supplier relationships, and more production capacity in the regions we already serve. Adjacent services for existing customers, which is how the brokerage came about. And selective acquisition through Pines Holdings, where buying an operating business is faster and less risky than building one.

Each division grows at its own pace, held to the same constraint: we do not take on volume the equipment and the crew cannot cover.

Leadership
Brant Borden
Founder, Pines Energy Group LLC

Brant has always been interested in entrepreneurship, particularly the kind that is useful to the community around it. Born in Mesquite, Texas, he visited family in East Texas often, and when his family moved to Hideaway he took to the area quickly. Over seven years in Hideaway and the surrounding towns he built friendships and working relationships across the community.

Hearing the people around him talk about fuel costs, he wanted to find something he could do about it. In a high school chemistry class he was introduced to transesterification. He read into the chemistry, ran experiments in his parents' garage, and worked out that the process could be run at a commercial scale.

The original business model was simple: collect used cooking oil from restaurants and convert it into biodiesel for local farms and ranches. That operation became the foundation for what is now Pines Energy Group LLC, and the same approach has carried into every division since. Find work that customers actually need done, do it reliably, and let the demand decide what comes next.
