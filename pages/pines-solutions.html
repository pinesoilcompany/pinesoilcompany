Starting Collection

  1. Contact Us

    Share your address and weekly oil volume.

  2. Container Placed

    We deliver a container sized to your kitchen.

  3. Scheduled Pickup

    Our crew collects and transports the oil.

  4. Recycled

    Oil is cleaned and sent to Pines USA for biodiesel.

Accepted

  • Used fryer oil (vegetable, canola, soybean, peanut)
  • Cooking grease and animal fats
  • Yellow grease

Not Accepted

  • Motor oil or any petroleum product
  • Water, chemicals, or degreasers
  • Trash or food scraps
  • Grease trap waste, unless arranged in advance

Who We Serve
