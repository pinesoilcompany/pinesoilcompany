The Company

One Group,
Four Operating Divisions

Pines Energy Group LLC was formed to hold and operate a set of related energy businesses under common ownership. Each division runs its own operations and serves its own customers, and each is accountable to the same standards for supply reliability, pricing transparency, and follow through.

The group started in fuel production and has grown into procurement, recovery, and ownership. Commercial buyers can source fuel through the brokerage, producers and fleets can work with Pines USA, food service operators can schedule collection through Pines Green, and business owners can talk to Pines Holdings about acquisition and investment.

We keep the structure simple on purpose. One point of contact, direct answers on availability and pricing, and a division that owns the work from the first call through delivery.
