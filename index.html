The Company

One Company, From Used Oil to Finished Fuel

Pines Energy Group LLC owns and oversees two operating companies. Pines Solutions recovers waste cooking oil from restaurants, food trucks, and other food service partners. Pines USA produces biodiesel from that oil and supplies it to commercial buyers.

Because both companies sit under one owner, oil collection, production, and delivery all run under the same management and the same standards. Whether you recycle oil with Pines Solutions or buy fuel from Pines USA, you are working with the same company.

How It Works

From the Fryer to the Fuel Tank

Every gallon of biodiesel Pines USA produces starts as waste cooking oil recovered by Pines Solutions.

  1. Collect

    Pines Solutions picks up waste cooking oil from restaurants and kitchens on a set schedule.

  2. Recover

    Food trucks and mobile partners bring their oil to the Recovery Point and pump it into secure storage.

  3. Clean

    Recovered oil is filtered to remove food solids and water before it moves on to production.

  4. Produce

    Pines USA turns the cleaned oil into biodiesel and supplies it in the blend each buyer needs.
