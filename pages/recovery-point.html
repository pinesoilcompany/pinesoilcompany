The Pines Recovery Point

A permanent, self-service site in Athens, Texas where registered food trucks and food service partners pump waste cooking oil into secure storage at any hour of the day.

What It Is

Built for Operators Who Move

Food trucks, caterers, and mobile kitchens rarely stay at one address long enough for a scheduled pickup. The Recovery Point gives them a fixed place to bring waste cooking oil whenever it suits their day, including early mornings, late nights, and weekends.

Partners pull up to the station, connect to the pump, and transfer their oil directly into an enclosed storage system. The oil is held securely on site until Pines Solutions collects it for cleaning and recycling. There is no open container, no waiting on a truck, and no oil left sitting behind your kitchen.

Pines Energy Group designed the Recovery Point to make waste oil collection secure, clean, and efficient for the people who produce it.

How It Works

Using the Recovery Point

Register once, then drop off whenever you need to.

  1. Register

    Contact Pines Solutions to register your business. Once approved, you receive your access details and site instructions.

  2. Arrive

    Come to 3855 TX-31 in Athens at any hour and pull up to the pump station.

  3. Pump

    Connect to the pump and transfer your waste cooking oil into the secure storage system, following the posted instructions.

  4. Go

    Disconnect, secure the station, and get back to work. Pines Solutions collects from storage and handles the rest.

Site Rules

Keeping the Site Safe and Clean

  • Only registered partners may use the station. Do not share your access details.
  • Waste cooking oil only. See the list of accepted materials on the Pines Solutions page.
  • Remove large food solids before pumping where possible.
  • Never add water, chemicals, degreasers, or petroleum products.
  • Follow the posted instructions and leave the station as you found it.
  • Report any spill, damage, or equipment problem right away by calling (512) 640-9102.
Why Partners Use It

Benefits

  • Always open. Drop off at 5 AM or midnight, weekdays or weekends.
  • No waiting. You do not have to hold oil on your truck until a pickup date.
  • Secure storage. Oil goes into an enclosed system, not an open drum or dumpster.
  • Clean handling. Direct transfer means fewer spills and less mess.
  • Put to use. Recovered oil is cleaned and made into biodiesel by Pines USA.

Questions

Recovery Point FAQ

Who can use the Recovery Point?

Food trucks, caterers, restaurants, and other food service businesses that have registered with Pines Solutions. The site is not open to the general public.

How do I register?

Send a message through our contact form or call (512) 640-9102. Tell us your business name and the type of operation you run. Once you are approved, we provide access details and site instructions.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Registered partners can use the station any time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How should I bring my oil?

Transport oil in sealed containers suited to carrying liquids, and let it cool completely before transfer. Your site instructions explain how to connect to the pump.

What if the pump is not working or I have a spill?

Stop and call (512) 640-9102. Do not try to repair the equipment yourself.

My restaurant cannot travel to the site. What are my options?

Pines Solutions offers scheduled on-site collection. We place a container at your location and transport the oil for you. See Scheduled Collection.
