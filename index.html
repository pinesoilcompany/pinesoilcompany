How It Works

  1. Collect

    Pines Solutions picks up oil from restaurants and kitchens.

  2. Recover

    Food trucks drop off oil at the Recovery Point.

  3. Clean

    Oil is filtered of food solids and water.

  4. Produce

    Pines USA turns it into biodiesel.
