Recovery Point

A 24-hour, self-service drop-off for waste cooking oil, built for food trucks and food service partners.

Register as a Partner Get Directions

Drop Off on Your Schedule

Food trucks rarely stay in one place long enough for a scheduled pickup. At the Recovery Point, registered partners connect to a pump and transfer oil straight into an enclosed, secured storage system. Pines Solutions collects it from there.

  • HoursOpen 24 hours, 7 days a week
  • AccessRegistered partners only
  • Address3855 TX-31, Athens, TX 75752
  • Support(512) 640-9102

How It Works

  1. Register

    Sign up with Pines Solutions and receive access details.

  2. Arrive

    Pull up to the pump station at any hour.

  3. Pump

    Transfer your oil into secure storage.

  4. Go

    Secure the station. We handle the rest.

Site Rules

  • Registered partners only. Do not share access.
  • Waste cooking oil only. See accepted materials.
  • No water, chemicals, or petroleum products.
  • Leave the station clean.
  • Report spills or equipment problems to (512) 640-9102.

Questions

Who can use it?

Food service businesses registered with Pines Solutions. It is not open to the public.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Registered partners can use it any time.

My restaurant can't travel. What are my options?

We offer free scheduled collection at your location.
