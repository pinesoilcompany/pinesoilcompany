Overview

Waste Oil, Handled Properly and On Schedule

Every commercial kitchen produces waste cooking oil, and all of it has to leave the building on a schedule that does not interfere with service. Pines Solutions handles that work, whether your kitchen stays in one place or moves from site to site.

Restaurants and kitchens that cannot travel are served by scheduled on-site collection: we place the container, run the route, and transport the oil for you. Food trucks and mobile operators can use the Recovery Point, our self-service drop-off in Athens that is open 24 hours a day.

Everything we collect is cleaned and sent to our sister company, Pines USA, to be made into biodiesel. None of it goes to a landfill or down a drain.

Scheduled Collection

We Come to You

For restaurants and kitchens that cannot make the trip to the Recovery Point, Pines Solutions collects on site and transports the oil for you. Your staff only needs to keep the container accessible on pickup day.

  • Container supplied, placed, and maintained, sized to your volume and space
  • Pickups planned around your service hours
  • Frequency adjusted when your volume changes
  • Extra pickups available on request
  • Collection records provided for your files
  • One agreement for multi-location groups and school districts

Cleaning and Recycling

What Happens to Your Oil

Collected oil is filtered to remove food solids and water, then held in storage until it is transferred to Pines USA for biodiesel production. Keeping collection, cleaning, and production in one company means the oil is handled through a documented process from your kitchen to the finished fuel.

Proper removal of waste cooking oil is also a health and code matter for food service operators. Collection records are available for your files.

Accepted Materials

  • Used fryer oil, including vegetable, canola, soybean, peanut, and similar oils
  • Cooking grease and animal fats from food preparation
  • Yellow grease

Not Accepted

  • Motor oil, hydraulic fluid, or any petroleum product
  • Water, cleaning chemicals, or degreasers
  • Trash, packaging, or food scraps
  • Grease trap or interceptor waste, unless arranged in advance

Not sure whether your material qualifies? Call us before your first pickup or drop-off.
