About Us

About Pines Energy Group

A Texas company that recovers waste cooking oil and turns it into biodiesel. We operate through two companies from our base in Athens, Texas.

Who We Are

Built Around a Single Resource

Pines Energy Group LLC is headquartered in Athens, Texas. The company began as a production operation converting used cooking oil into biodiesel for local farms and ranches, and it has grown by following what that work needed.

Producing biodiesel reliably required a steady supply of oil, which became a collection operation serving restaurants and commercial kitchens. Food trucks and mobile operators did not fit a fixed pickup route, which led to the Recovery Point, a self-service site they can use at any hour. Those operations are now organized as two companies: Pines Solutions, which recovers and cleans the oil, and Pines USA, which produces and supplies the fuel.

Each company has its own customers and its own work. What they share is ownership, a single point of contact, and a commitment to doing what was agreed, when it was agreed.

Operating Principles

How We Run the Business

Fuel and oil collection are services customers depend on. We compete on whether the work gets done when promised, whether the price is fair, and whether someone answers when something changes.

Do What Was Agreed
A pickup date or delivery window is a commitment, not an estimate. If we cannot meet one, you hear it from us first.
Price It Openly
Quotes are explained plainly. Customers who understand how a price is built make better decisions.
Keep the Structure Flat
Small teams and direct lines. You deal with people who know your account and can act on it.
Control the Supply Chain
Running our own collection is what makes production consistent, and consistency is what buyers pay for.
Say No When It Fits
If biodiesel is wrong for your equipment or a route does not make sense for your volume, we will tell you.
Document the Work
Collection records and batch documentation are kept and provided on request, so you can verify what you were told.
Company Development

How the Company Grew

Growth follows the customers we already have. When an account needs something we do not yet offer, that is the signal to look at adding it. It is a slower way to expand, and it puts real demand behind every investment.

We do not take on more volume than our equipment and our crew can handle.

  1. Formation

    Biodiesel Production Begins

    The company is founded in East Texas to convert used cooking oil into biodiesel for local farms and ranches.

  2. Feedstock Supply

    Collection Routes Established

    Scheduled collection from restaurants and commercial kitchens secures a consistent oil supply. This work becomes Pines Solutions.

  3. Recovery Point

    Self-Service Drop-Off Opens

    The Recovery Point opens in Athens so food trucks and mobile operators can recycle oil on their own schedule.

  4. Group Structure

    Pines Energy Group LLC Organized

    Operations are organized under Pines Energy Group LLC, with Pines USA handling fuel and Pines Solutions handling oil recovery.

Leadership

Brant Borden

Founder, Pines Energy Group LLC

Brant has always been drawn to entrepreneurship, particularly the kind that is useful to the community around it. Born in Mesquite, Texas, he visited family in East Texas often, and when his family moved to Hideaway he took to the area quickly. Over seven years in Hideaway and the surrounding towns he built friendships and working relationships across the community.

Hearing the people around him talk about fuel costs, he wanted to do something about it. A high school chemistry class introduced him to the chemistry behind biodiesel. He studied it further, ran experiments in his parents' garage, and worked out that the process could be run at a commercial scale.

The original business model was simple: collect used cooking oil from restaurants and turn it into biodiesel for local farms and ranches. That operation became the foundation for Pines Energy Group LLC, and the same approach carries through both companies today. Find work customers actually need done, do it reliably, and let demand decide what comes next.
