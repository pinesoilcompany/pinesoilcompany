Common Questions

Will biodiesel work in my equipment?

Most diesel engines accept low blends like B5, and many accept B20. Approval for higher blends varies by make and model year. Check your manufacturer's guidance, and we can help you choose.

How does cold weather affect it?

Biodiesel thickens at higher temperatures than petroleum diesel. Lower blends and proper storage handle most Texas winters. Ask us before the season changes.

How is it priced?

By blend, volume, and delivery location. Contact us for a current quote.

Can I get batch documentation?

Yes. Batch records are available on request before purchase.
